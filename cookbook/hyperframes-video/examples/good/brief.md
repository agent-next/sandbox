# Brief: Aurora Notes product teaser

Make a 20-second product teaser video for **Aurora Notes**, a notes app.

- Format: 1920x1080 at 30 fps, silent.
- Brand colours: indigo `#4F46E5` (primary), amber `#F59E0B` (accent) on a
  dark slate `#0F172A` background.
- Beats, on screen in this order:
  1. Title — "Aurora Notes" (from t=0.4, hold 2.6 s)
  2. Beat 1 — "Capture ideas in one keystroke" (from t=3.4, hold 4.0 s)
  3. Beat 2 — "Sync across every device" (from t=7.8, hold 3.8 s)
  4. Beat 3 — "Search everything instantly" (from t=12.0, hold 4.0 s)
  5. End card — "Try it free today" (from t=16.4, hold 3.4 s)
- Every beat must be fully legible at its timestamp; no black gaps between
  beats; brand colours visible in every beat; no text clipped or
  overflowing its container.
- Keep something moving throughout — no frozen holds, no static frames.
  Motion should be the kind HTML/CSS/JS does well: staggered kinetic
  typography, a UI that types and filters itself, animated numbers.
