# Brief: Aurora Notes product teaser

Make a 24-second product teaser video for **Aurora Notes**, a notes app.

- Format: 1280x720 at 30 fps, silent.
- Brand colours: indigo `#4F46E5` (primary), amber `#F59E0B` (accent) on a
  dark slate `#0F172A` background.
- Beats, on screen in this order:
  1. Title — "Aurora Notes" (from t=0.5, hold 3.0 s)
  2. Beat 1 — "Capture ideas in one keystroke" (from t=4.0, hold 4.0 s)
  3. Beat 2 — "Sync across every device" (from t=9.0, hold 4.0 s)
  4. Beat 3 — "Search everything instantly" (from t=14.0, hold 4.0 s)
  5. End card — "Try it free today" (from t=19.0, hold 4.0 s)
- Every beat must be fully legible; no black gaps between beats; brand
  colours visible in every beat.
- Keep something moving throughout — no frozen holds, no static frames
  (an animated progress bar counts).
