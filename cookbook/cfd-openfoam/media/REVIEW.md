## 7. Visual fixes applied (this commit)

**Issue 1 — Cavity |U| field (result.png panel d and video frames):**
- Before: drawn as separated scatter squares with white gaps (marker='s', linewidths=0 on unstructured centres).
- After: continuous cell field via `tripcolor` with `shading='gouraud'` on the real cell centres (Delaunay triangulation), plus streamlines from `streamplot` (Ux/Uy interpolated onto a 61×61 regular grid via `griddata(method='linear')`). The primary vortex is now clearly visible. Applied to both `make_figures.py` (panel d) and `make_frames.py` (every video frame).

**Issue 2 — result.png panel (b) PASS box overlap:**
- Before: green box at `ax.text(0.03, 0.06, ...)` (lower-left in axes coords) covered the v-curve and Ghia squares near x = 0.6–0.85, y = –0.2.
- After: moved to `ax.text(0.03, 0.90, ..., va='top')` (upper-left inside the axes). Verified no overlap: the data lives in the lower/right portion; the box sits in the clear upper-left corner. Legend also moved from `upper center` to `lower right` to avoid any collision.

**Issue 3 — loop.png panel (a) pass-line annotation crosses PASS bar:**
- Before: `ax.annotate(xy=(3.4, 2.0), xytext=(1.35, 12))` — arrow cut through the PASS label of the last bar (index 3).
- After: annotation target moved to `xy=(3.7, 2.0)` (near right edge of the pass line), text to `xytext=(2.8, 18)` (well above the PASS bar). Added a visible green dot at the PASS bar's real value with the numeric value printed: `ax.plot(pass_idx, errs[pass_idx]*100, 'o', ms=8, color=GREEN, ...)` and `ax.text(..., '%.2f%%' % (errs[pass_idx]*100))`.

**Issue 4 — Video frames: title and layout:**
- Before: `t = ... s` title floated in top-right with large empty area; residual panel used only bottom half of right side.
- After: one figure-level title (left-aligned) `fig.text(0.04, 0.96, 'Lid-driven cavity, Re = 100 | t = ... s', ...)`. The |U| field takes the left ~55% (`ax = fig.add_axes([0.04, 0.06, 0.52, 0.88])`), and the residual panel uses the full right ~45% height (`ax_res = fig.add_axes([0.60, 0.06, 0.38, 0.88])`). Streamlines added to every frame. Residual panel pass-line label moved to `transform=ax_res.transAxes` at (0.02, 0.92) so it never overlaps the curves.
