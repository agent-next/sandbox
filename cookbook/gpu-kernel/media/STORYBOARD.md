# gpu-kernel demo video — storyboard

`demo.mp4` is 31 s, 1920x1080, 30 fps, H.264 yuv420p CRF 18, moov before
mdat. Every number on screen is read from real judge output: this sandbox's
recorded run (`media/clips/*.json`, gitignored, regenerated by
`record_clips.py`) or the committed GPU run (`media/data/report.json`,
`RECEIPT.md`). The renderer is Remotion (`media/video/`, headless Chrome);
ffmpeg only encodes the master and the web file, it draws nothing.

## Rules this cut follows

- Every shot before the end card moves: no still plot, no text-only frame,
  no Ken-Burns over a still. The two chart shots grow for the whole time they
  are on screen and keep a sweeping cursor after they finish growing.
- The terminal footage is a real screen recording of the real commands, not
  a re-type: `record_clips.py` captures the run with timestamps and
  `render_clips.py` replays it at the captured pace, sped up and labelled.
- The proof is a HUD over still-playing footage (slot 6), not a slide.
- The last 2.5 s is the only static frame.
