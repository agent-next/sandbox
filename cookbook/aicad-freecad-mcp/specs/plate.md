# Part spec — adapter plate

Make a rectangular adapter plate 100 × 80 × 10 mm, centred on the origin.
Central through bore Ø30 mm. Six through holes Ø5.5 mm on a bolt circle of
R35 mm centred on the origin in the plate plane, first hole at 0° from the
+X axis, then every 60°. Units mm. Deliver STEP, binary STL and a DXF top
view (outline + all circles, no dimensions or text).
