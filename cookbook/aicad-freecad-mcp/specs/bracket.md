# Part spec — L-bracket

Make an L-bracket. Base plate 80 × 50 mm, 10 mm thick, x 0→80, y 0→50,
z 0→10. Upright occupying x 70→80, y 0→50, z 0→60. Fillet R8 mm on the
inside corner where the base top face meets the upright inner face. Two Ø10
mm through holes in the base, axes vertical, at (25, 25) and (55, 25). Two
Ø12 mm through holes in the upright, axes along X, at y = 15 and y = 35,
z = 45. Units mm. Deliver STEP and binary STL.
