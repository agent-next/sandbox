# Part spec — flanged bushing

Make a flanged bushing. Axis vertical (+Z) through the origin. Through bore
Ø20 mm over the full height. Hub Ø36 mm, 40 mm tall. Flange Ø60 mm at the
bottom, 8 mm thick. Units mm. Deliver STEP and binary STL.

All edges must be sharp (no chamfers, no fillets). The bore must be
concentric with the hub and flange.
