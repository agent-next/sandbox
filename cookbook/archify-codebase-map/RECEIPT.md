## Deviations

- **archify release age:** v3.0.1 (2026-09-28) is pinned instead of the
  newer v3.1.0 (2026-10-13) to honour "at least two weeks old".
- **Node 24 vs 18 on PATH:** the builder box shadows apt's Node 18 with
  a `/usr/local/bin/node` v24. `setup.sh` detects this, pins
  `/usr/bin/node` (v18.20.4) into `~/.archify_demo_node`, and
  `check_gates.py`/`selftest.sh`/`make_media.sh` all read that marker.
  Archify behaves identically on both (pure JS, no native bindings) —
  verified byte-for-byte renders on each.
- **diagram.html is git-ignored:** archify output is ~800 KB of embedded
  fonts/JS; the judge regenerates it from `diagram.json`, and the PNGs in
  `media/` carry the visual.
- **Ground truth is a clone, not the tarball:** archify `--repo-root`
  requires a `.git` directory. The sha256-verified tarball is kept as the
  integrity baseline and `setup.sh` diffs the cloned `httpx/` tree
  against it file-for-file before proceeding.
