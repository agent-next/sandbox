# demo.mp4 storyboard -- firmware-qemu

The owner rejected the previous cut as "the ppt video": it was matplotlib
scenes and still figures with a fade between them.  **This cut has no
slides.**  Every second before the end card is real recorded output of this
demo -- the firmware booting in QEMU, the toolchain compiling it, the judge
grading it, the judge killing mutants -- played back as moving terminal
footage, composed in HyperFrames, with animated overlays whose numbers come
from the same run the footage came from.

## Source of truth

* Footage: `media/clips/*.cast` -- asciicast v2 recordings made by
  `media/record.py` from a real run (see `media/clips/README.md`).
  Regenerated by `media/make_media.sh`; `media/term.py play` replays each
  to 30 fps frames and ffmpeg encodes.
* Numbers: `media/data/verdict-good.json` (8/8 gates, 46/46 shell, 15/15 CRC,
  .text+.rodata 2224 B), the selftest summary in `selftest.cast` (14/14
  mutants, 100 % kill rate), `arm-none-eabi-size` in `build.cast`
  (FLASH 2744 B of 256 KB, SRAM 4456 B of 64 KB) and `media/RECEIPT.md`.
  No number on screen is typed by hand into the composition.

## Rules this cut follows

* No text-only frame, no figure shown as a still, no Ken-Burns: every
  pre-end-card frame is a frame of a real recording, played or panned.
* Cuts: at least 0.8 per 10 s (the gate's floor; the eight slots above cut
  about 8 times in 31.5 s).
* Motion: the gate is 0.20, our own bar is 0.50.  Achieving it needs every
  pre-end-card clip to keep moving, so slots 4/5/7 pan the footage (the
  terminal is a moving window over a real recording) as well as animate the
  overlay.
* Camera moves are zoom/pan of the real recording -- never a synthetic
  zoom on a still.

## Status

then encodes.  Every claim here is checked by
`cookbook/tools/video_gate.py` (and `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs that
gate's test).
