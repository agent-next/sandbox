L-bracket.

- L cross-section in the XZ plane through the points
  (0,0), (50,0), (50,10), (10,10), (10,60), (0,60), extruded 40 mm in Y.
- Legs 10 mm thick.  Overall 50 wide × 60 tall × 40 deep.
- One R 5 mm fillet on the re-entrant inner corner (the corner at
  (10,10)), running the full 40 mm depth.
- 2 × Ø 5 mm mounting holes per leg, through the 40 mm depth:
  vertical leg: axes at (x=5, z=35) and (x=5, z=55);
  horizontal leg: axes at (x=25, z=5) and (x=45, z=5).
- All dimensions in mm.
- Frame: hole axes parallel to Y; profile in z = 0..60.
