Adapter plate.

- Plate: 60 mm × 60 mm × 8 mm thick, centred on the Z axis.
- Centre bore: Ø 12 mm straight through.
- 4 × Ø 6.5 mm through holes on a Ø 44 mm bolt circle,
  first hole at 45°, then every 90° (45/135/225/315°).
- All dimensions in mm.
- Frame: plate spans z = 0..8; hole axes parallel to Z.
