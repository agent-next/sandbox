Questions

Where does the forecast come from?

The national weather service hourly model, 1 km grid, refreshed every three hours.

Does it work for running?

Yes. The wind math is the same; you set your still-air speed.

What if the forecast is wrong?

Kestrel shows the confidence band and suggests the most sheltered loop.

Can I export to my bike computer?

GPX for every device, with wind notes as course points.

Do I need an account?

No. The first five routes are planned on this device.
