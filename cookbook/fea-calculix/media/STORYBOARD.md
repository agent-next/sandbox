# demo.mp4 storyboard — fea-calculix

Every second before the end card is real moving output of this run: the
CalculusX field is re-rendered per frame by `render_clips.py` (orbiting
orthographic camera over the boundary faces of the exact `mesh.inc` the
solver consumed, painted with the nodal von Mises field ccx wrote to
`job.frd`), and the terminal clip scrolls this sandbox's own console
logs. HyperFrames (`media/video/index.html`, generated by
`gen_composition.py`) composes the clips and adds the animated overlays.
ffmpeg only encodes.

Numbers on screen come from `data/verdicts/good.json`,
`data/verdicts/overload-10x.json`, `data/selftest.txt` and
`work/run_logs/timing.json` — injected by `gen_composition.py`, never
typed.
